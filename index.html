01 Skills, Tools & Education

Tools I Currently Use

Ableton Live 12 is my main DAW. I write and record with both acoustic and electric guitar. My current recording setup is simple and non-standard (electric guitar into a ¼-inch to 3.5 mm converter, then into the line-in on my PC), but it works for the stage I am at and allows me to capture ideas and develop them.

Skills I Feel Confident With

  • Understanding the structure and workflow of Ableton Live
  • Writing simple guitar riffs and basic lyrics
  • Arranging and warping recordings
  • Manual MIDI drum programming
  • General computer and software troubleshooting

Areas I Am Actively Improving

  • Vocal mixing and performance
  • Creating clean transitions
  • Frequency balance and avoiding muddiness
  • Overall mix cohesion and fine-tuning of arrangements

Education

  • Cambridge IGCSE – Completed
  • Cambridge AS Levels – Currently completing
  • SAE Institute – Bachelor of Arts in Sound Production (Applicant)

I am not currently employed and am focusing on finishing my AS Levels while preparing my application for SAE. My comfort with computers and software has helped me teach myself production tools and solve technical problems as they arise.

03 About & Personal Statement

I am a self-taught songwriter, producer and performer. I have been actively creating music for over a year, building on an earlier interest that began in my youth with simple tools like BeepBox. What started as experimentation has grown into a real passion for sound design, arranging, and shaping music from the ground up.

I currently have a growing collection of original tracks. I write guitar melodies and riffs, arrange them to the best of my ability with the equipment I have, perform my own vocals, and create drum patterns that support the feeling of each song. While my mixing and vocal performances are still developing, I am constantly listening, learning, and trying to improve.

I am applying to the SAE Bachelor of Arts in Sound Production because I want to turn this passion into a professional career working with sound. I am especially drawn to sound design, the process of recording and refining tracks in a DAW, and learning how to create strong, clear sound in both studio and live environments. Self-teaching has taken me a certain distance — now I want structured training, real experience, and the technical foundation needed to grow further.

My goal is simple: to develop the skills that will let me work confidently with sound and share music that people can truly feel.

What I Bring

  • Complete creative ownership — writing, production and performance
  • Clear evidence of self-directed improvement across a body of work
  • Strong motivation and readiness for professional training
  • Genuine curiosity about sound design and how sound works

04 My Learning Journey & Passion

I have always felt a deep emotional connection with music. It is the way I process and express what I feel, and it is also something I want to share — both through my own songs and by eventually helping other people bring their ideas to life when they cannot produce them themselves.

Everything I know so far has been self-taught. I work in Ableton Live 12, play guitar, and handle my own mixing. I programme drums manually by drawing MIDI notes, and I continually practise balancing elements so they sit together more cleanly. Over the past year I have focused on improving arrangement, sound design, and the overall cohesion of my tracks.

The areas I still find most challenging are vocal mixing, creating smooth transitions, and placing sounds in the mix without them becoming muddy or fighting for space. Frequency clashes and level balance remain things I am actively working to understand better.

I believe SAE will give me the proper technical foundation I cannot fully build on my own. Structured training in recording, mixing, sound design, and live sound will help me develop the skills and confidence needed to work professionally and open doors that would be much harder to reach by myself.
